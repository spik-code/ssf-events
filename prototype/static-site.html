The name

SSF Events — Sri Sai Flora Enterprises

SSF Events is the decor arm of Sri Sai Flora Enterprises, working out of Kolar and taking functions across the district and into Bengaluru, Bangarpet, Avani and Tumkur.

We are a hands-on team: the people who design your stage are the people who build it that morning and take it down that night. Flowers, structure, drapes, lighting and sound come from one point of contact, so nothing falls between vendors on the day.
